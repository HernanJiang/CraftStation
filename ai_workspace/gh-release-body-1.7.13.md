## CraftStation v1.7.13

Skill names accept underscores; quieter thread rows.

- Skills named with underscores (my_interview, _private_skill) are no longer filtered out — they import cleanly, pass validation, and bind through / or $ invocation in the composer.
- Sidebar thread rows show their full title by default; the pin, more, and archive actions stay hidden until the row is hovered or focused, matching the project rows.
