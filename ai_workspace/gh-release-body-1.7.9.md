## CraftStation v1.7.9

Update badge clears after a landed update. A small fix for the update indicator that kept showing a pending update after the CLI had already been updated.

- The titlebar update badge no longer stays lit after a CLI update lands — the post-update re-check now compares against the freshly detected version instead of a stale snapshot.
