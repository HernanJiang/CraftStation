## CraftStation v1.7.8

A real follow-up queue & tidier thread header. Queueing a message while a thread is working now stacks every message instead of silently replacing the previous one.

- Queue as many follow-up messages as you like — each gets its own row above the composer and sends in order when the turn settles, with Send now, Edit and Delete per message.
- A failed send restores the message at the front of the queue instead of dropping it.
- The pane close button moved to the far right of the thread header, after the pop-out control.
